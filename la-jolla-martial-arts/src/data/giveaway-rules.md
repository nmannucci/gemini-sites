# Official Rules

## La Jolla Martial Arts "Win a FREE Year of Kids Martial Arts" Giveaway

**NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE DOES NOT IMPROVE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED. OPEN ONLY TO RESIDENTS OF ZIP CODES 92037, 92117, 92109 AND 92122.**

### 1. Sponsor

The giveaway is sponsored by **La Jolla Martial Arts, LLC**, 7680 Girard Ave, Basement, La Jolla, CA 92037, (858) 361-0961, LJBULLYPROOF@GMAIL.COM ("Sponsor").

### 2. Who can enter

You can enter if you are:

- the parent or legal guardian of a child who is **3 to 12 years old** on the day you enter;
- at least 18 years old; and
- living in ZIP code **92037 (La Jolla), 92117 (Clairemont), 92109 (Pacific Beach) or 92122 (University City)**.

Current and former La Jolla Martial Arts students may enter. Employees of Sponsor and members of their households may not.

### 3. One entry per family

Each family may enter **once, for one child**. A family is identified by phone number and email address. If you submit the form again, your new answers replace your earlier entry. Submitting again does not add a second entry or improve your chances.

### 4. Entry period

Entries open {{entry_open}} and close {{entry_close}}, Pacific Time. Sponsor's server clock is the official clock for this giveaway. Entries received outside this period don't count.

### 5. How to enter

Go to **lajollatkd.com/giveaway** and answer every question: your name, mobile number, email, your child's first name and age, your ZIP code, the benefits you'd like your child to gain, and whether your child can attend at least twice a week. Then submit the form.

**To enter, you must agree to receive text messages from Sponsor about this giveaway**, as described in section 11. Your answers about benefits and attendance help us plan. They do not affect your chances of winning.

Entries that are incomplete, false, or submitted by automated means are void.

### 6. Grand prize

One winner receives the **1-Year Martial Arts Scholarship** for the child named in the entry:

| Included | Approximate retail value |
|---|---|
| Three kids martial arts classes per week for 12 consecutive months | $2,640 ($220 per month) |
| One La Jolla Martial Arts uniform | $80 |
| Registration | $70 |
| **Total approximate retail value** | **$2,790** |

The winner pays $0 for the items above.

**Not included:** belt testing fees, sparring gear and other equipment, tournaments and special events, private lessons, birthday parties, transportation, and anything else not listed above. If the child takes part in any of these, the family pays Sponsor's normal price.

**Prize conditions:**

- The prize must be claimed in person within five days (see section 8). The 12 months begin on the day it's claimed.
- Classes follow Sponsor's published class schedule for the child's age group.
- The prize can't be exchanged for cash, transferred to another child, or combined with other offers. Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a listed item becomes unavailable.

### 7. Drawing and odds

On {{drawing_date}}, Sam Hadaf of La Jolla Martial Arts will randomly draw one winner from all eligible entries received during the entry period. The result is subject to verification of eligibility. Your chances of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

### 8. Winner notification

Sponsor will contact the winner directly by phone, text message, and email, and will announce the winner to entrants by text message on {{announcement_date}}, Pacific Time. The claim period starts from that direct notice. **To claim the prize, the winner must bring the child to a class at La Jolla Martial Arts in person within five (5) days** and sign a declaration confirming eligibility. If the winner doesn't claim the prize in person within five days, is found ineligible, or declines the prize, the prize is forfeited and Sponsor may draw an alternate winner from the remaining eligible entries. Each alternate also has five days from Sponsor's notice to claim in person.

### 9. Taxes

The winner is responsible for any taxes on the prize. Before awarding the prize, Sponsor will ask the winner for a completed IRS Form W-9 and will report the prize's value to the IRS on Form 1099 as required by law.

### 10. The $600 Scholarship for entrants who don't win

**This is a promotional offer, not a prize.** It is not awarded by chance, and receiving it does not mean you won the giveaway.

Separately from the drawing, Sponsor offers every eligible entrant who does not win the grand prize a **$600 Scholarship toward one year of martial arts** for the child named in the entry. Terms:

- **How it applies:** $600 off 12 months of kids martial arts tuition, as either a single payment of **$2,040** for 12 months or **$170 per month** ($50 off each month for up to 12 months). The uniform and registration are included at enrollment.
- **How to claim it:** after the winner is announced, Sponsor will send the scholarship notice by email and text with a claim deadline five days later. Schedule and **attend** a free introductory class in person by that deadline. Scheduling a class without attending does not claim the scholarship.
- **Who can use it:** children who are not currently enrolled at La Jolla Martial Arts. It can't be applied to an existing membership. Former students who are not currently enrolled can use it.
- **What it isn't:** it has no cash value, can't be transferred to another child or family, and can't be combined with other offers. One per family.
- Full enrollment terms, including payment schedule, cancellation, and pricing after the 12 months, are provided in writing before you enroll. You never have to enroll to stay in the giveaway.
