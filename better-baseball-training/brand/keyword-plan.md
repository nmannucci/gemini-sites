### D-BAT Rocklin
- Strong on "baseball and softball training", memberships, batting cages, lessons, camps, clinics, and pricing.
- Their pricing page gives concrete lesson and membership numbers, which makes comparison searchers easier to capture.
- BBT opportunity: create clearer value and development-path pages that go beyond cage rental and broad franchise positioning.

### Hard 90 Baseball
- Strong in El Dorado Hills for youth programs, private lessons, power hitting, power pitching, power catching, power infield, camps, and travel teams.
- Their site has many program-specific pages.
- BBT opportunity: compete with cleaner location pages, coach-specific trust, and parent-decision guides.

### Sportstime Baseball Academy
- Strong for El Dorado Hills/Folsom/Cameron Park private lessons, clinics, and professional/college coaching proof.
- BBT opportunity: win with year-round indoor facility, academy membership, and 8-14U recurring development language.

### Alpha Athletics EDH
- Strong positioning around "all training needs under one roof", facility classes, teams, softball, and strength/performance.
- BBT opportunity: sharpen youth baseball-specific pages and avoid being too generic.

### Sacramento BPA
- Strong travel baseball and team-development positioning, including youth and high-school programs.
- BBT opportunity: separate "travel baseball development" from "tryout/program logistics" and capture younger 8-14U parent searches.

### Ace Pitching
- Narrow pitching specialist for Sacramento, El Dorado Hills, Folsom, Roseville, Loomis, and Rescue.
- BBT opportunity: build a dedicated pitching lessons page around Jean Machi, Gabe Emmett, arm care, mechanics, sequencing, and youth development.
