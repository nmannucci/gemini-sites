## Summer Baseball Camps at St Joseph High School

The Technique Tigers Baseball Academy Summer Camps offer a fantastic environment for baseball players ages 6-12 to hone their skills. Our camps are designed to develop the fundamental skills of baseball and help campers grow in their knowledge of the game. We cover pitching, fielding, hitting, and base running with an emphasis on improving technique and reinforcing the basics. At our camp, campers will have the opportunity to practice their newly acquired skills while they interact with our knowledgeable coaches and enjoy a great atmosphere. Our camp offers several different age-specific levels so that campers of any ability can participate and feel comfortable. We emphasize the importance of teamwork and sportsmanship throughout the entire camp experience.
