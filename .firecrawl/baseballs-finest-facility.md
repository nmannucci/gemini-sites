### _**Team/Player Training Rental Rates**_

**![](https://i0.wp.com/www.baseballsfinestnj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/BFLogo.jpg?resize=240%2C87&ssl=1)Home to the New Jersey Tribe Travel Baseball Club we also host many schools and travel athletic programs.  Accommodating all ages and skill levels, our 5,000 square foot indoor turf facility has 60 foot retractable batting/pitching cages, allowing for pitching at any level. Net dividers separate the entire facility in half or split the tunnels allowing for multiple athletes to practice. The well-lit warehouse space has no pillars or support beams making it an excellent area for team work, larger group practices/clinics and extensive defensive work. Baseball’s Finest also features a gym making it truly one step ahead. The entire 5,000 indoor facility, tunnels and personal training gym are available for rent as well as single tunnels by the hour. _Coach Conway is available to assist all team/group rentals upon request for no additional charge._**

**Indoor Baseball/Softball Training Facility features:**

- Wall-to-Wall Turf
- Two 60 ft Retractable Hitting Tunnels
- 60 ft x 50 ft Open Area with no pillars/support beams
- Baseball Pitching Machine
- Gym with wide variety of equipment, free weights, resistance bands and more
- Coach Conway instruction and all other instructors upon availability

_**Rental Practice Sessions can include additional instructors specializing in Baseball or Softball: hitting, pitching, catching, infield, outfield and/or baserunning**_ **.**
