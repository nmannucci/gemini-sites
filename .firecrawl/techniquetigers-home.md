## What Make Us Different?

Tenure, tradition and continuity. Technique Tigers Baseball Academy is the longest running baseball academy in Fairfield County CT. Our professional staff has over 100 plus years of playing/coaching experience. Our main focus is that all players receive a comprehensive baseball program that is going to nurture baseball players into successful student athletes.

Developing skills sets consistently over time requires commitment, structure and discipline. Commitment to practice regimens weekly over months is required to join our program. All of our practices are driven by repetitions, structure and professional instruction. The Technique [Tigers](https://maillettechiropractic.com/) Baseball Academy has a long tradition of developing responsible community leaders. Our family atmosphere has allowed us to create a culture of continuity, players that land into the academy find a home to develop skills mentally/physically that will carry on for a lifetime. Our coaching staff is original and has worked diligently over the last 16 plus years to build a brand in our community that is reputable and respected.

## Matt Batten

![](<Base64-Image-Removed>)

Matt Batten 32nd Round Pick to Major League Player Matt Batten is living proof that hard work, dedication, and a little bit of luck can take you far.

Batten was originally a 32nd-round pick out of Quinnipiac University in the 2017 Major League Baseball Draft. After being drafted, Batten made the commitment to work hard and make it to the big leagues, and this past summer, he did just that. This June, Batten’s contract was selected by the San Diego Padres where he spent 58 days in the big leagues this year. He still remains on their 40-man roster. Batten’s journey to the big leagues began even before he was drafted in his hometown of Shelton, Connecticut. At Technique Tigers Baseball Academy Batten worked on perfecting his skills and honing his craft to prepare for the next level. At Quinnipiac University, Batten is the All-Time hits leader. He continued to work on his strength and conditioning to make sure he was ready for the rigors of professional baseball. Batten credits much of his success to the Technique Tigers Baseball Academy. He believes that the Academy helped him become the player he is today and helped him reach his goal of [playing](https://swaviation.net/) in the Major Leagues.
