## Corrections made to the preview

- Added the official phone number and made it callable throughout the site.
- Replaced an unsupported general-hours claim with accurate program-specific schedule language.
- Added Fort Moore to the service area and location pages.
- Aligned program names and descriptions with the official private instruction, summer academy, Hitting Legacy, Pitching Legacy, Catcher's Legacy, and strength-and-conditioning pages.
- Clarified that the website emphasizes baseball while the academy works in several sports.
- Added the published Catcher's Legacy prerequisite instead of implying that every program is open to every new athlete.
- Removed generic template claims about drills, equipment, availability, camps, and team training that were not supported by the official website.
- Avoided invented pricing, testimonials, coach biographies, roster details, hours, and registration availability.
